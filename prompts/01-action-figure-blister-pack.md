# Action Figure in Blister Pack

Category: Edit image prompt

Input: One clear front-facing photo of yourself (or a colleague, with permission).

## Prompt

```text
Create a hyper-realistic photo of an action figure of the person
in the uploaded image, sealed inside a retail blister pack.

PACKAGING: Glossy plastic blister bubble over a printed cardboard
backing. Backing color: bold yellow with a thin red border.
HEADER (top of card): the name "JERRY — SENIOR iOS DEV" in bold
sans-serif, with a small tagline below: "Caffeine sold separately."

FIGURE: 1/6 scale, faithful likeness, same outfit as the photo,
neutral standing pose, articulated joints visible.

ACCESSORIES (molded in clear plastic next to the figure):
- A tiny MacBook Pro
- A small coffee cup with steam decal
- A "TestFlight build #42" loose paper card

LIGHTING: Bright, even toy-store shelf lighting. Soft reflections
on the plastic bubble. Background slightly out of focus, suggesting
a retail aisle.

ASPECT RATIO: 3:4, vertical.
```
