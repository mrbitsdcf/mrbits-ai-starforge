# Meet Your Younger Self — Cat Edition

Category: Edit image prompt

Input: Two photos of the same white cat — one from when it was a kitten (or younger age) and one recent photo. Front-facing photos work best for the most natural result.

## Prompt

```text
Using the two uploaded images, create a single composed black-and-white
portrait of the same cat at two ages, sharing one frame.

LEFT SIDE: The childhood version (from image #1), facing slightly
right toward the adult, soft expression.

RIGHT SIDE: The present-day adult version (from image #2), facing
slightly left toward the child, calm half-smile, looking down at the
child with warmth.

INTERACTION: The adult's hand rests lightly on the child's shoulder.
Both figures share the same warm directional light from the upper-left.

BETWEEN THEM: A small birthday cake on a simple wooden stool, one
single lit candle, faint smoke trail.

BACKGROUND: Plain soft studio backdrop, gradient dark gray to black.

STYLE: Black and white, fine film grain, cinematic studio portrait.
Slight vignette. Aspect ratio: 4:5.

DO NOT alter the faces or identities of either cat — preserve
both likenesses exactly as in the uploaded photos.
```

## Expected Output

A black-and-white image (4:5 aspect ratio) in a cinematic studio portrait style, showing the same cat at two ages side by side — kitten on the left, adult on the right — with subtle interaction between them (paw on shoulder) and a small birthday cake with a candle in the center.

## Limitations

- Requires two photos of the same cat at different ages; photos of different cats may produce visual inconsistencies.
- Front-facing photos work best; side angles make the side-by-side composition difficult.
- The "hand on shoulder" instruction is anthropomorphic — the model may interpret this in various ways depending on the cat's pose.
- The prompt is specific to white cats; adapt the description for other breeds/colors.

## Examples

**Input:** Photo 1: 3-month-old white kitten, front-facing. Photo 2: same cat as a 4-year-old adult, front-facing.

**Expected result:** B&W studio portrait with the kitten on the left looking slightly right and the adult on the right looking down warmly, cake with candle between them, dark gray gradient background with slight vignette and film grain.
