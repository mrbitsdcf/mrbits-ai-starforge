# Magazine Cover With Real Typography

Category: Generate image prompt

Input: No upload required.

## Prompt

```text
Magazine cover illustration, vertical A4 portrait, designed for
newsstand display.

TITLE TYPOGRAPHY (top third of frame):
The headline "NORTH WIND" in massive carved cross-section wood-grain
serif letters, stacked two lines — "NORTH" on top, "WIND" below.
Letterforms show concentric tree-ring patterns inside each glyph.
Deep oxblood red on aged cream paper. Hand-pressed woodcut feel,
slight registration drift, edges chipped.

SUBJECT (centered, ¾ length, mid-frame):
A 32-year-old woman in a hand-knit fisherman's sweater the color of
unbleached wool, salt-stained leather jacket open over it, copper
hair pulled into a loose low braid, calm tired half-smile, faint
freckles across her nose.

LIGHTING: Overcast coastal daylight, soft key from upper-left, cool
fill from below, faint sea-spray haze in the background.

CAMERA: Eye-level, 85mm portrait lens feel, shallow depth of field,
subject perfectly sharp, background soft.

LOWER LEFT CAPTION (small serif type):
"Issue No. 17 — Heirloom Wool, Borrowed Boats."

LAYOUT: Title dominates upper third, subject fills lower two-thirds,
caption tucks into lower-left. Poster ratio 3:4.

MOOD: Quiet, weathered, slightly melancholic.
```

## Expected Output

A vertical (3:4) editorial magazine cover illustration, with carved wood-grain typography ("NORTH WIND") in the upper third, a woman in coastal attire in the center/lower two-thirds, and a small serif caption in the lower-left corner. Quiet, weathered, slightly melancholic aesthetic.

## Limitations

- No upload required; the prompt describes a fictional subject.
- The wood-grain tree-ring typography is complex — models may simplify the internal letter details.
- In-image text ("NORTH WIND", caption) may not be 100% legible depending on the model used.
- To adapt the theme, edit the TITLE TYPOGRAPHY, SUBJECT, and LOWER LEFT CAPTION sections.
