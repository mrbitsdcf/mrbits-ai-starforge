# 90s Yearbook Polaroid

Category: Generate image prompt

Input: No upload required.

## Prompt

```text
A 1990s high school yearbook portrait photograph. Single subject,
centered, head-and-shoulders crop.

SUBJECT: A 17-year-old skater with messy bleached-blond hair and
dark roots, oversized graphic t-shirt for a fake band called
"PIER 9", silver chain necklace, faint scar on his eyebrow,
slightly forced smile showing braces with blue rubber bands.

BACKGROUND: Classic studio yearbook backdrop — laser-grid pattern
over a teal-to-magenta gradient. Slight vignette on the corners.

LIGHTING: Hard direct flash from the camera, slightly washing out
skin tones, sharp shadow behind the head.

PHOTO QUALITY: 35mm film grain, slightly overexposed, faded color,
muted reds and greens, tiny dust speck near the upper-right corner.
Soft focus around the edges.

POLAROID FRAME (optional): White Polaroid border, slightly off-square,
with handwritten ballpoint pen text in the bottom margin:
"Marcus — 1996 — picture day."

ASPECT RATIO: 4:5.
```

## Expected Output

A vertical (4:5) image simulating a 90s school yearbook photo in Polaroid format. Fictional subject (17-year-old skater) centered with direct flash, 35mm film grain, faded colors, and a teal-to-magenta gradient background with a laser-grid pattern. White Polaroid frame with handwritten text in the bottom margin.

## Limitations

- No upload required; the subject is described in the prompt.
- Handwritten text on the Polaroid frame may be illegible or contain spelling errors depending on the model.
- The "90s aesthetic" (laser-grid, faded colors) can vary between models — some may produce more modern-looking results.
- To customize the subject, edit the SUBJECT section with the desired description.

## Examples

**Prompt usage:** Paste the prompt into an image generator with no additional upload.

**Expected result:** Yearbook-style photo with bleached-blond skater with dark roots, oversized "PIER 9" t-shirt, silver chain, smile with blue braces, teal/magenta laser-grid background, visible film grain, Polaroid frame with "Marcus — 1996 — picture day." written in pen.
