# Minimalist Brand Logo — the one that replaces a Fiverr gig

Category: Generate image prompt

Input: No upload required.

## Prompt

```text
Logo design presentation sheet for a small independent coffee
roaster. Pure white background, perfectly centered logo, plus a
small black-and-white variant below.

COMPANY: "NORTHERN STILL"
INDUSTRY: Specialty coffee roaster, small-batch, direct-trade.
AUDIENCE: Adults 25–45, design-conscious, prefer minimalism over
ornament.

PRIMARY MARK (top, large):
- A geometric symbol on the left: a single coffee bean rendered as
  two overlapping crescent shapes, balanced and symmetrical, drawn
  with hairline strokes only (no fill).
- The wordmark on the right: "NORTHERN STILL" in a custom geometric
  sans-serif, all caps, generous letter-spacing, perfectly aligned
  to the symbol's vertical center.
- Color: deep forest green (#1F3A2E) on white.

SECONDARY MARK (bottom, smaller, monochrome):
- Same logo in solid black on white, no green.

CONSTRAINTS:
- No drop shadows, no gradients, no 3D, no clipart, no coffee cup
  illustrations, no steam swirls.
- Hairline strokes only. Geometric. Modernist.
- All text crisp and exactly readable.
```

## Expected Output

A logo presentation sheet image on pure white background, with the primary mark ("NORTHERN STILL") in forest green (#1F3A2E) at the top (geometric coffee bean symbol + geometric sans-serif wordmark) and a smaller monochrome variant in black below. Minimalist style, no gradients, shadows, or decorative illustrations.

## Limitations

- No upload required; the prompt describes the full visual identity.
- Typographic precision (letter-spacing, alignment) is highly model-dependent — manual refinement may be needed.
- The geometric symbol (two overlapping crescents, hairline) is subtle and may be simplified or distorted by some models.
- To adapt for another brand, edit COMPANY, INDUSTRY, AUDIENCE, and the PRIMARY MARK description.
