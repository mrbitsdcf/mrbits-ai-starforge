# Infographic Poster

Category: Generate image prompt

Input: No upload required.

## Prompt

```text
Editorial infographic poster, vertical A3 portrait, modular card layout.

HEADLINE (top, large sans-serif): "WHY YOUR APP IS SLOW"
Subhead (one line below, smaller serif): "Five culprits, ranked by
how often they show up in real audits."

CARD 1 (top-left): Icon of a magnifying glass over a list. Label:
"Unbatched network calls." Stat callout: "37% of cases."

CARD 2 (top-right): Icon of an overflowing folder. Label:
"Oversized image assets." Stat callout: "26% of cases."

CARD 3 (middle-left): Icon of two arrows colliding. Label:
"Main-thread blocking work." Stat callout: "18% of cases."

CARD 4 (middle-right): Icon of a leaking faucet. Label:
"Retained closures and memory leaks." Stat callout: "12% of cases."

CARD 5 (bottom, full width): Icon of a stopwatch. Label:
"Inefficient layout passes." Stat callout: "7% of cases."

FOOTER (bottom, very small):
"Source: 200 audits, 2024–2026 · Made with ChatGPT Images 2.0"

STYLE: Modern editorial information design. Pale cream background.
Charcoal black text. One accent color — terracotta orange — used
only for stat callouts. Hairline rules between cards. Tiny spot
illustrations, not photos.

TYPOGRAPHY: Serif headline, sans-serif body. All text crisp and
readable.

ASPECT RATIO: 2:3, vertical.
```

## Expected Output

A vertical (2:3) infographic poster with modular card layout, sans-serif headline ("WHY YOUR APP IS SLOW"), 5 cards with icons, labels, and stat callouts in terracotta orange, on a pale cream background. Legible typography, modern editorial style with hairline rules between cards.

## Limitations

- No upload required; statistical data is fictional and illustrative.
- Text legibility depends heavily on the image model — long text or statistics may become unreadable.
- The modular 5-card layout may be interpreted differently by each model (sizing, positioning).
- To adapt the theme, edit the headline, the 5 cards, and the footer with the data source.

## Examples

**Prompt usage:** Paste the prompt into an image generator with no additional upload.

**Expected result:** Vertical A3 poster with cream background, "WHY YOUR APP IS SLOW" title at the top, 5 cards arranged (2+2+1) with minimalist icons, black labels, and percentages highlighted in terracotta orange, discreet footer at the bottom.
